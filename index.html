Inside Ziri's World

Four worlds. One floor.

Every corner of the park is soft-padded and built around a different kind of play.

Ball Pit & Slides

A giant ball pit with a wide multi-lane slide, a twisting tube slide and a net tunnel overhead.

Ziri Toddlers

A mini town of soft blocks, role-play houses and a see-saw, made for the smallest explorers.

Brew Corner

The coffee-shop corner at the foot of the clock tower, right beside the toddler town.

Flower Lounge & Games

Flower tables, a book wall and a big game screen for calmer moments between slides.
