Before You Visit

Good to know

🧦 Socks required

Grip socks are required for all guests on the play floor. Bring your own or buy a pair at the desk (AED 10).

👨‍👩‍👧 Adult entry

One accompanying adult per paying child enters free. Extra adults can relax in the café area at no charge.

🚗 Parking

Free parking is available directly in front of the venue — no time limit during opening hours.

📅 Walk-ins welcome

No booking required on weekdays. We recommend booking online for Friday and Saturday sessions.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us how often you plan to visit and we'll point you to the most affordable option.
