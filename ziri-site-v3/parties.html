Good to Know

Party FAQ

Can we bring our own cake?

Absolutely — bring your own cake at no extra charge. We provide candles and a full cake-cutting service.

What's the minimum group size?

Packages are built around the headcounts shown, but smaller groups are welcome. Contact us for a custom quote.

Do parents get floor access too?

Yes — socks on, parents are welcome on the floor. Those who prefer to watch can use the parents' lounge area.

How far in advance should we book?

Weekends fill up fast — we recommend booking at least 2 weeks ahead, especially for Friday and Saturday slots.

What if we need to cancel or reschedule?

Contact us at least 72 hours in advance and we'll reschedule your party at no charge.

Ready to pick a date?

Tell us the headcount, the theme, and the date — we'll confirm availability within one business day.
